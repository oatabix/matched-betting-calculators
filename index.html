Bonus wagering: what to check

For matched bonuses, note whether wagering is on the deposit, bonus, or both; which games contribute; maximum stake and cashout cap; expiry; and whether your deposit must be lost first. A simple RTP-based EV estimate can overstate the value of a bonus when your balance can run out before wagering completes.

Keep medium- and high-risk offers separate until we build a calculator that models balance exhaustion, wagering requirements and withdrawal caps.
